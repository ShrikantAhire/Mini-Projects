## Episode 1

**Title:** How David Goggins Built Willpower From Nothing | Willpower Ep 1

**Description:**

At 24, David Goggins weighed nearly 300 pounds, could barely read, and worked nights spraying for cockroaches. He went on to become a Navy SEAL and set a world record with 4,030 pull-ups in a day. He says he did it with no natural talent.

In episode 1 of this 3-part series, you'll learn:
- Why Goggins runs on the "stick" instead of the "carrot", and why motivation fades so fast
- What science says (and doesn't say yet) about the part of the brain linked to sticking with hard things
- Why willpower fades if you stop training it
- The one daily habit that builds willpower, with 4 simple rules you can start tomorrow
