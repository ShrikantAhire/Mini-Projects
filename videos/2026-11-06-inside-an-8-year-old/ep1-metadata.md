## Description

```
Why does a happy 8-year-old suddenly start asking "Am I good enough?"
Follow Leo through one week of school and see it the way he sees it: class photo day, a spelling test, and a lunch table with no seat left.

Around age 7 or 8, children start judging themselves by comparing with the kids around them. It's a normal stage, and it's why a 7 out of 10 can suddenly feel small. Episode 1 shows what's going on inside a child's mind, what the research says, and three things parents and teachers can start tonight.

Inside an 8-Year-Old's Mind is a 3-part story series for kids, parents and teachers. Episode 2, The Club, is next.
```
